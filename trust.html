Trust & security

Deal context is sensitive. We treat it that way.

Negotiation briefs can include term sheets, pricing, and strategy. ShadowProxy is early-stage software; our security posture starts with clear data handling, encryption, and least-privilege access.

What we collect

Waitlist / demo emails and the negotiation context you send us. When the product is in use: scenario inputs you provide to run a simulation, and generated briefs. We do not sell personal or deal data.

Encryption

TLS in transit. At rest, customer and scenario data are planned for encrypted storage (e.g. S3 / DB with KMS) as we harden the AWS stack.

Access control

Least-privilege IAM for cloud workers. No long-lived keys in the browser. Human access to inquiry mail is limited to the founding team.

Data separation

Raw customer deal text is kept separate from any training corpora. We do not use your private scenarios to train public models without explicit consent.

Retention

Inquiry emails are kept only as long as needed to respond and operate early access, then deleted or anonymized.

Compliance path

We are pre-incorporation / early prototype—not SOC 2 certified yet. Near-term focus: encryption defaults, audit logging on the AWS data plane, and written retention rules before enterprise pilots.

Your controls

  • Request access, correction, or deletion of personal data by email.
  • Ask us not to retain a scenario after a demo.
  • Review our legal pages before sharing sensitive materials.
