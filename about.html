About ShadowProxy

ShadowProxy is a Negotiation / Strategy AI project: multi-agent simulation that helps technical founders rehearse pricing, sales, and deal conversations before they walk into the room.

Why this project exists

Many strong technical builders struggle in the conversations that set the value of their work— pricing a product, selling to a counterpart, defending terms on a term sheet. They know the technology; they do not always have a structured way to rehearse the room.

ShadowProxy was started to give those people a simulation-backed rehearsal: describe the negotiation, explore likely counters with multi-agent search, and leave with ranked next moves—so better products are not underpriced or undersold.
