Why we exist

High-stakes conversations—term sheets, partnership pricing, commercial deals— decide whether a product finds its market. Many strong builders still walk into those rooms underprepared: they know the tech, but not how to price it, defend value, or navigate a counterpart who negotiates every day.

ShadowProxy exists to give technical founders and deal teams a rehearsal space: describe the situation, simulate likely counters, and leave with ranked next moves— so better products are not underpriced or undersold in the room that matters.
